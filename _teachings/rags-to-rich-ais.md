I taught this course as a **Subject Matter Expert** through [PESU I/O](https://pesuio.pes.edu/), PES University's peer-teaching program, to a class of 27 students.

The course covers retrieval-augmented generation from the ground up and then pushes past the naive version of it:

- **RAG architecture** - chunking, embeddings, vector stores, and why the retrieval step is usually where things break
- **Advanced RAG techniques** - reranking, query rewriting, hybrid search, and evaluation
- **GraphRAG** - building and querying knowledge graphs when flat similarity search isn't enough
- **LLM agents** - building agentic systems with LangChain, LlamaIndex, and CrewAI

The emphasis throughout was hands-on: students built working systems rather than only reading about them.
