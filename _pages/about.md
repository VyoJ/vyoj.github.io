I'm interested in **computer vision** and building **systems for AI**. My work so far has been vision-language models for documents in languages the field has left behind, and the data and inference infrastructure they depend on.

I helped build **Nayana** at [CognitiveLab](https://www.cognitivelab.in/): a family of document-centric vision-language models trained on synthetic data. That meant producing over 2 million pages across 22 languages, then using it to train [Nayana-OCR](https://aclanthology.org/2025.lm4uc-1.11/) and the [NetraEmbed](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.03514) retrieval models. Nayana won Meta's Llama Impact Grant in 2025, one of ten globally.

Alongside that I've worked on document parsing pipelines at [Sarvam AI](https://www.sarvam.ai/), synthetic data for robot manipulation at [IIIT Bangalore](https://www.iiitb.ac.in/), porting [llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp) to IBM z/OS mainframes, and AI-assisted issue triage at [Apple](https://www.apple.com/). My capstone, [HyperCluster](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-27358-1_2), shards LLMs across a self-organizing peer-to-peer cluster of edge devices.

I also worked on [manimator](https://huggingface.co/spaces/HyperCluster/manimator), which turns research papers into animated explanations (featured in Hugging Face's Spaces of the Week). Outside of work, I organize quantum computing events as a Qiskit Advocate, and I've taught RAG and LLM agents to undergraduates through PESU I/O.

I'm always looking for new research opportunties, so if any of this overlaps with what you're looking for, please do reach out!
